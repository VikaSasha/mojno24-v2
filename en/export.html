Export

The whole range, shipped to four countries

We supply partners in Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Kyrgyzstan. Every item in the catalogue is available for export.

Products

For importers

Ready for EAEU and CIS markets

  1. Multilingual labels

    Soup packs carry Russian, Belarusian and Kazakh text; RisoMishki boxes also Uzbek, Azerbaijani and Chinese.

  2. EAEU declarations

    Soups and porridges: ЕАЭС N RU Д-RU.РА04.В.18188/25, valid until 11 May 2030. RisoMishki: ЕАЭС N RU Д-RU.РА03.В.74745/26, valid until 17 May 2031. Other products — on request.

  3. Halal certificate

    Street Lunch soups — РОСС RU.33039.ИЛ05ОС.00119, valid until 25 March 2028.

  4. Export packs

    Most often we ship display boxes (25 sachets) and doypacks (5 × 40 g sachets); all other packs are also available.

  5. 12-month shelf life

    Enough time for logistics and sales: soups, RisoMishki and Khrumstik keep for a year.

  6. Free samples

    We send samples for tasting and quality checks before the first order.
