About us

Making food from grains since 2003

Novye Produkty is a food manufacturer from Chelyabinsk, Russia. We believe healthy food can also be quick: a hot soup without a stove in three minutes, a crispy dessert with tea, a grain bar on the go.

Approach

Grains and pulses at the core

Everything we make is based on grains and pulses: peas, lentils, rice, buckwheat, spelt, wheat and barley, sourced from Russian farmers.

Peas and lentils become purée soups that need no stove. Puffed rice becomes crispy teddy bears in glaze. Puffed grains become bars sweetened with fructose. Products are made to our own technical specifications and covered by EAEU declarations of conformity.
