Private label

Our product, your brand

We build a private-label line on our own technology: from recipe and samples to packaging with your logo.

What we can make

Four product directions

Instant soups & porridges

Pea and lentil purée soups, buckwheat porridge. Sachets, boxes, cups, doypacks.

Puffed-rice desserts

Shaped crispbread in glaze, or salted — like RisoMishki.

Puffed-grain bars

With fructose, nuts, seeds and dried fruit.

Grains & flour

Plain grains and pulse flours, packed for your chain.

Case

RisoMishki for ZhiznMart

Since late 2024 we have been producing RisoMishki for the Russian retail chain ZhiznMart under a co-branded label — white and milk chocolate, with a dedicated box design agreed with the chain.

    We adapt one of our products and send samples.

  4. Documents

    Specifications, declarations, label data.

  5. Production

    We make the batch and deliver.
